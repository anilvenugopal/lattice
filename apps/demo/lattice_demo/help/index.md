---
title: Demo
summary: How to use the Work Queue, Jobs and Inbox screens.
---

The demo app shows the three shapes of the Lattice grid. Every list screen in
every Lattice app works the same way, so what you learn here applies everywhere.

| Screen | Use it to |
|---|---|
| [Work Queue](work-queue.md) | Review, assign and decide on incoming items |
| [Jobs](jobs.md) | Watch background jobs, and retry or cancel them |
| [Inbox](inbox.md) | Read, star and archive your messages |

> [!TIP]
> Press **F1** on any page to open the help for that page, and **Ctrl J** to search
> pages, records, commands and help from anywhere.
