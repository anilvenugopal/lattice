**Items** is a list of sample records with every kind of column: references,
badges, people, progress bars, money, percentages, tags, dates and a star.

## Find items

- **Tabs** (Open, Mine, Starred, Done, All) are views: preset filters.
- **Search** matches the reference, name, owner, tags and country.
- **Filters** narrow by status, priority, category and owner.
- **Views ▾** saves what you have set up under a name, to open again later or
  share with everyone. See [Working with lists](/help/lattice/user-guide/working-with-lists).

## Act on items

Select rows, then use the bar above the list:

- **Assign** and **Set status** ask for a value first.
- **Complete** applies only to active and blocked items; others are skipped.
- **Star** toggles the star; click the star in a row to do the same.
- **Delete** needs the **Delete items** permission, which only administrators
  have at first.

Click a row to see it in the side panel; double-click or press **Enter** to
open its record page.

## The record page

An item's page shows its status, the same actions as the list, its details,
a lifecycle stepper, its notes, its child items as a tree, the other items in
its category, and its history.

## Create and edit

**New item** and **Edit** use an ordinary form. Leave the name empty to see a
validation message; saving returns you to the item with a confirmation.
