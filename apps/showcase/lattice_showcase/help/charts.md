The **Charts** page draws the Items list ten ways: bars, areas, lines, a
doughnut, a calendar heatmap, a treemap, a flow diagram and a world map.

- **They follow the list.** Choose a tab, filter or search in the list below
  the charts, and every chart redraws for the same items.
- **Zoom** a time chart by dragging across it; **Reset zoom** goes back.
- **Hover** for exact values.
