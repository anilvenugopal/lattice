Claims come from the claims system, the system of record. This app keeps what
the response adds: zones, surveyors, desk handlers, priority, assessments and
field reports. **Open in claims system** on a claim leads back to it.

## The list

- **Tabs**: Open, Mine (claims you handle), Waiting for a surveyor, Priority,
  and All.
- **Search** matches the claim number, insured, policy, city, county, zone,
  surveyor and handler.
- **Draw** on the map to narrow the list to an area, or **find a place** and
  keep the claims within a distance of it; the charts follow.
- Select claims, then **Assign surveyor**, **Move to zone**, **Take** (become
  their desk handler), **Priority** or **Inspected**.

## A claim's page

- **Status**, from new to closed.
- **How long each stage took**: waiting for a surveyor, with the surveyor,
  settling and closing, as a timeline, with field reports as diamonds.
- **Damage assessment**: the form changes with what was inspected (a home, a
  vehicle or a commercial building). Saving it marks the claim inspected.
- **Field reports**: what the surveyor saw, in their own words.
- **Coverages and payments**: the estimate split across the policy's
  coverages.
- **Where**: the location on the map, inside the event's footprint.
- **Claims within 1 mile** and **Locations within 5 miles**: the lists, narrowed
  to the neighbourhood around this claim.
- **Comments**: a comment tells the claim's desk handler, in their
  [notifications](#following-a-claim).

## Field reports

Surveyors write **free-form** reports: a summary and what they observed, plus
any field they need (a locked gate, a dog on site). The **Field reports** page
lists them beside a reading pane.

## Live intake

New claims for Hurricane Delta arriving from the claims system. In the
showcase they come from a **simulator**: **Start intake** at 10, 30 or 120
claims a minute, and **Pause**. Use 120 to show a surge.

## Deployment

Each zone of the live event, its lead, its surveyors and their claims, as a
graph. Select a zone to **prioritise its waiting claims**. Click a surveyor to
open their claims in the zone.

Below it, **Surveyor visits** shows each surveyor's inspection visits through
the day. Bars stacked on top of each other are visits booked at the same time:
a surveyor with more work than hours.

## Following a claim

**Follow** on a claim's page tells you about changes to it: a new comment, for
example, reaches you under the bell in the top bar. Taking a claim (**Take**)
follows it for you, so the desk handler hears about every comment. See
[Notifications](/help/lattice/user-guide/notifications) for the bell, the list
and clearing them.
