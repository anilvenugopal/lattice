**Locations** is the exposure snapshot: every insured location, with its
policy, line, construction, insured value and location quality.

## Search on the map

Use the drawing tools on the map: a **polygon**, a **rectangle** or a
**circle**. The list, the totals and the charts narrow to the locations inside
it. The shape stays in the address and in a saved view, so you can share it.

The map shows the insured value **as hexagons**, so concentrations stand out
whatever the county lines. Zoom in to see each location, coloured by its
location quality.

## Find a place

Type a town, county or zip code in the map's search box and pick it. The map
drops a pin and the list narrows to locations **within 5 miles**; change the
distance beside the box (1 to 50 miles). The chip **Within 5 mi of Lake
Charles, LA** clears it. A claim's page links here with the pin on the claim:
**Locations within 5 miles**.

## Save as area

**Save as area** keeps the shape under a name, such as "Evacuation zone 3".
Use it as the affected area when you [declare an event](events.md#declare-an-event).

## Filters

Line, construction, location quality and state; search matches the location,
insured, policy number, city and county.
