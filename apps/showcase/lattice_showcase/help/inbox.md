The Inbox shows only your own messages. The list is on the left and the selected
message on the right. Opening an unread message marks it as read.

## Folders

Use the sidebar to switch between **Inbox**, **Starred** and **Archive**.

## Actions

- **Mark read** and **Mark unread** change the bold unread marker.
- **Star** marks a message to find it later; click the star in the list to toggle it.
- **Archive** moves messages out of the Inbox, and **Move to Inbox** brings them back.
- **Delete** removes messages for good.

Use **↑** and **↓** to move through messages; the reading pane follows.
