## Specs

**Specs** lists documents. Opening one shows a form drawn from its schema:

- **Lists** such as Tags and Dimensions have **Add**, move up, move down and
  remove on each entry.
- **Made by** switches between a supplier and an internal team; the fields
  below change with it.
- **Other fields** lists values the schema doesn't describe. They are kept as
  they are.
- **JSON** shows and edits the whole document as text.

Saving checks every field and shows a message at each one that needs fixing.

## Free-form

**Free-form** edits a document that has no schema: the form follows whatever
the document holds. Add a field with **Add field** at the bottom.
