---
title: Flows
summary: A stepper, a tree and a dependency graph you can act from.
order: 60
keywords: flow stepper tree graph dependencies nodes
---

- **The stepper** shows steps in order: done, current, failed, waiting or
  skipped.
- **The tree** shows items and their child items; open and close each branch.
- **The graph** shows which items depend on which. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom.
  Select an item to see **Open** and its actions (**Complete**, **Star**,
  **Delete**); double-click it to open its page.
