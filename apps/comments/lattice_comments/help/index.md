Comments work on records from every app: the **Comments** panel on a record's
page shows its comments, oldest first.

## Comment on a record

Type in the box at the bottom of the **Comments** panel and choose
**Comment**. Writing comments needs the **Write comments** permission, which
editors have; everyone with **Read comments** sees them.

The app that owns the record may tell its owner about your comment. In the
showcase, a comment on an item puts a message in the item owner's Reading
pane.

## Delete a comment

The bin beside a comment deletes it. You can delete your own comments;
holders of **Moderate comments** can delete anyone's.

## All comments

**Comments** in the rail lists every comment, newest first, with the record
each is about. Search by text, author or record.
