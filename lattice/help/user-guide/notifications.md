The **bell** in the top bar shows how many notifications you have not read.
Select it to open **Account › Notifications**.

## What you are told about

- **Records you follow.** Select **Follow** on a record's page to hear about
  changes to it: a new comment, for example. Select **Following** to stop.
- **Things an app tells you directly**: a claim or an incident assigned to you,
  say. Apps may follow records for you when you take them on.

A notification says what happened and links to the record. You see the record
only if you still may: one you can no longer open shows as unavailable.

Several notifications of the same kind about the same record are folded into
one, with a count, until you read it.

## Clearing them

- **Open** one: it is marked read and takes you to the record.
- **Mark read**, **Mark all read** and **Dismiss** in the list.
- Some clear themselves when their record is resolved or closed.
- They are deleted after 90 days.

The **Unread** tab lists what you have not read; **All** lists everything. The
home page counts your unread notifications.
