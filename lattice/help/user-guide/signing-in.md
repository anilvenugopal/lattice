## Sign in

The sign-in page offers one or both of these, depending on how your site is
set up:

- **Continue with …** (for example, Continue with Microsoft) signs you in
  through your organisation's single sign-on. Use it if you can.
- **Email and password** signs you in with an account created for you by an
  administrator.

After signing in you land on your start page: the home page, unless you chose
another one in [Your account](your-account.md#preferences).

## Change your password

If an administrator created your account or reset your password, you are asked
to choose a new one the first time you sign in.

To change it later, open **Account › Security** and choose **Change password**.

A password needs:

- at least 12 characters;
- an upper-case letter;
- a lower-case letter;
- a digit.

Changing your password signs you out everywhere else.

If you sign in with single sign-on, you have no password. **Account › Security**
offers **Set password** if you want one as a fallback.

## Access pending

If you see **Access pending**, you are signed in but have no role yet, so there
is nothing you can open. Ask an administrator to grant you a role. Once they
have, sign out and in again.

## Signing out

Open the menu under your initials, top right, and choose **Sign out**, or
press Ctrl J and run **Sign out**.

You are also signed out automatically:

- after a period without activity (8 hours, unless your site changed it);
- a set time after you signed in (24 hours, unless changed);
- when you change your password elsewhere;
- when an administrator signs you out or deactivates your account.

To see where you are signed in, and to sign out other devices, use
[Account › Security](your-account.md#sessions).
