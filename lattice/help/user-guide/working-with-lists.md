Every list of records works the same way, in every app. Once you know one, you
know them all.

## Find the rows you want

- **Views** are the tabs above the list, such as **Open**, **Assigned to me**
  and **All**. Each is a saved set of filters.
- **Search** with the box above the list (press **/** to jump to it). Every
  word you type must match, in any of the searchable columns.
- **Filters** are the buttons next to the search box. Choose one, then tick
  the values you want. A filter with a value chosen shows a count.
- **Sort** by choosing a column heading. Choose it again to reverse the order.
- **Pages.** The footer shows how many rows match and moves between pages.
  **Rows** sets how many rows a page shows.

The address in your browser keeps the view, search, filters, sort and page.
Copy it to share exactly what you see, or bookmark it.

## Save a view

Save a search and filters you use often, and open them again with one click.

1. Set up the list: choose a tab, search, filter and sort.
2. Open **Views** (next to the columns icon) and choose **Save current view…**.
3. Name it and choose **Save**.

The **Views** button then shows the view's name. Open **Views** and choose a
saved view to return to it.

- **Change a view.** Open it, change the search or filters, then choose
  **Save changes to "…"** in **Views**. The pencil renames it; the bin deletes
  it.
- **Share a view.** Tick **Share with everyone who can open this list** when
  you save it, or later with the pencil. Shared views appear under **Shared
  with everyone** for everyone who can open the list, with the owner's name.
  Only the owner changes a shared view. Sharing needs the **Share saved views**
  permission, which editors have.
- **What is saved:** the tab, search, filters, sort and rows per page. Columns
  are your own choice for the list, saved separately.

## Look at a row

- **Choose a row** to open its side panel with the details, without leaving
  the list. ↑ and ↓ move to the next row and the panel follows. **Esc**
  closes it.
- **Double-click a row**, or press **Enter**, to open its full page, where the
  list has one.

In a list with a reading pane, such as an inbox, the pane is always open on
the right.

## Select rows and act on them

| To select | Do |
|---|---|
| One row | Tick its box, or press Space with the row highlighted |
| Several rows | Tick each box, or Ctrl-click (⌘-click) |
| A range | Click the first row, then Shift-click the last |
| The whole page | Tick the box in the header, or press Ctrl A |
| Everything that matches | Select the page, then choose **Select all N matching** |

When rows are selected, the **action bar** shows what you can do with them,
such as **Assign**, **Approve** or **Delete**. The same actions are in each
row's **⋯** menu.

- **Greyed out actions.** An action is greyed out when it does not apply.
  Hover over it to see why: for example, "Select exactly one row".
- **Partly eligible selections.** If only some selected rows qualify, the
  action runs on those and tells you how many it skipped.
- **Confirmations.** Some actions ask you to confirm, or ask for a value first,
  such as who to assign to.
- **Results.** A message at the bottom right says what happened.
- **Activity.** Every action is recorded in the record's activity history.

You only see the actions your roles allow.

## Columns and export

- **Columns** (the columns icon) shows or hides columns. Your choice is
  remembered for that list.
- **Export CSV** (the download icon) saves the rows that match your view, search
  and filters as a CSV file, for Excel or other tools.
- **Refresh** (the circular arrows) reloads the rows. Some lists, such as jobs,
  refresh themselves.
