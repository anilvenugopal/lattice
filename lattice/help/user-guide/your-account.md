Open your account from the person icon at the bottom of the rail, or the menu
under your initials, top right. It has three pages.

## Preferences

| Preference | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Theme** | Light, Dark, or Match system (follows your computer's setting) |
| **Density** | Comfortable, or Compact for more rows on screen |
| **Times** | Relative ("3h ago") or Absolute ("17 Oct 2026 14:05"). Times are always shown in your computer's time zone |
| **Start page** | The page you land on after signing in |
| **Rows per page** | How many rows lists show at first: 25, 50, 100 or 200 |

Choose **Save preferences**. They follow you to any computer you sign in from.

To switch theme quickly, use the moon or sun icon in the top bar, or press
Ctrl J and type "theme".

## Profile

**Profile** shows your name, email, roles and how you sign in.

- **Your name.** Change it and choose **Save**.
- **Your email** is your sign-in name. Only an administrator can change it.
- **Your roles.** Ask an administrator if you need a different one.

## Security

### Password

**Change password** asks for your current password and a new one. If you sign
in with single sign-on, **Set password** adds a password as a fallback.
Changing your password signs you out on every other device.

### Sessions

**Active sessions** lists every browser where you are signed in, with when it
was last used. The one you are using is marked **This device**.

- **Sign out** on a row ends that session.
- **Sign out all other sessions** ends every session except this one.

If you see a session you do not recognise, sign it out, change your password,
and tell your administrator.
