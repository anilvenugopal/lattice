Lattice is the platform this site runs on. It provides:

- sign-in;
- the navigation around every page;
- Ctrl+J search;
- your preferences;
- the lists and forms that every app shares.

> [!TIP]
> Press **F1** on any page to open the help for that page, and **Ctrl J** to
> search pages, records, commands and help from anywhere.

## Using Lattice

The [User guide](user-guide/index.md) covers signing in, finding your way, working with lists and
your account.

## Building on Lattice

The [Developer guide](developer/index.md) explains how Lattice works and how to build, run and
extend apps on it. Start with the [Overview](developer/overview.md).
